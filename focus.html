The Problem

Focus is
a business problem.

This is not a discipline problem. The mind is constantly in a switch state — trying to do everything at once. The cost is felt not just by the individual, but quietly, persistently, by the entire organisation.

Stress rises. Team performance degrades. Meetings drift. Decisions drag. People arrive at the end of a day that felt busy — but didn't feel purposeful.

47 sec
The Attention Span
The average time before a person switches screens. Not by choice — by conditioning. Every notification has trained the brain to move, not stay.
2 min
The Interruption Window
How long a knowledge worker goes before being interrupted. Deep work — the kind that actually moves things forward — becomes structurally impossible.
23 min
The Recovery Cost
The time the brain needs to return to full focus after a single interruption. Multiply this across a workday, and the cost becomes staggering.

"That's why we built something different. Not a workshop. Not a talk. An experience designed to make focus felt."

Oru Cup Focus · The Clinic
The Experience

Focus through
all five senses.

Oru Cup Focus takes each participant through 6 stations across the five senses — one from each sensory zone, plus a workplace focus challenge. Each station is a short, self-paced attention exercise, guided only by audio.

The clinic runs across multiple variants, so no two participants go through exactly the same set. No screens. No lectures. No group performance pressure. The result is a felt map of where each person's attention actually lives.
